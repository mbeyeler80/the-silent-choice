# The Silent Choice — Prog 70s Music Pack v0.2

Original prototype score with restrained 1970s progressive-rock influence:
modal harmony, Mellotron-like textures, organ, analog synth, sparse odd meters and long quiet passages.

The goal is not retro pastiche. The prog vocabulary should feel like an archaeological musical layer inside a posthuman cyber-spiritual world.

Use smooth ~2 s crossfades. Keep music low under text. Scene 4 should remain close to silence.
